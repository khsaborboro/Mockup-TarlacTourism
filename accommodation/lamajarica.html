01 — WHERE TO STAY

La Maja Rica Hotel

La Maja Rica Hotel is a premier destination for comfort and elegance in Tarlac City. Featuring well-appointed rooms, a relaxing pool area, and a popular in-house restaurant, it offers a blend of modern amenities and warm hospitality. Conveniently located near major highways, business centers, and local attractions, La Maja Rica is perfect for travelers seeking both relaxation and accessibility in the heart of the city.

Available Room Types

Main Wing Room Deluxe Suite Executive Pool View

TYPE OF ROOMS

Main Wing Rooms

Main Wing - Executive Single


Tailored for a relaxing and productive stay, this spacious guest room features a comfortable double bed, a dedicated work desk, a vanity area, and an HDTV with an array of HD channels. Enjoy full pool access along with complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and daily breakfast to enhance your experience.

Main Wing - Executive Double


Designed for optimal family comfort and relaxation, this spacious guest room features two plush double beds, a dedicated work desk, a vanity area, and an HDTV with an array of HD channels. Enjoy full pool access along with complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and a daily breakfast to make your stay truly unforgettable.

Main Wing - Executive Triple


Designed to comfortably accommodate families or groups, this inviting guest room features three single beds, a dedicated work desk, a vanity area, and an HDTV with a broad selection of HD channels. Enjoy complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi and a daily breakfast to ensure a truly refreshing and convenient stay.

Main Wing - Junior Suite


Designed for special occasions and memorable family getaways, this sophisticated and well-appointed suite features a plush queen-sized bed, an elegant anteroom, and a striking full-length mirror. Offering the unique flexibility to connect with the Executive Triple Room for expanded family space, it provides the ideal setting for wedding preparations and relaxing family staycations alike.

Premier Tower Rooms

Premier Tower - Standard


Experience one of our most beloved spaces, where luxurious comfort meets everyday functionality. Whether you are here to unwind or stay productive, you will have everything you need right at your fingertips—from a sleek HDTV and refreshing mini-bar to a spacious work desk and fast, complimentary Wi-Fi.

Premier Tower - Superior


Perfectly balancing luxury and everyday comfort, this room is designed with your relaxation in mind. Enjoy a refreshing dip in the bathtub and take advantage of modern conveniences, including a premium HDTV, mini-bar, work desk, and complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi.

Premier Tower - Double


Elegantly decorated in soothing tones, our Premier Double rooms are impeccably furnished to provide a warm, residential feel. Offering significantly more space than a standard guest room, this inviting retreat gives you the perfect environment to stretch out, unwind, and truly relax.

Premier Tower - Presidential Suite


As the pinnacle of luxury among our accommodations, the Presidential Suite offers an unparalleled guest experience. Pamper yourself in a private bedroom featuring a plush king-size bed, unwind in your spa-inspired bathroom complete with a Jacuzzi, and enjoy the expansive comfort of a separate living area and a beautifully appointed mini-kitchen.
