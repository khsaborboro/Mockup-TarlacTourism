FOOD &
DINING

Known as the melting pot of Central Luzon, the Province of Tarlac serves up a rich tapestry of Kapampangan, Ilocano, and Pangasinense flavors. Savor everything from the famously crisp Chicharon Camiling and sweet Iniruban to hearty heritage dishes crafted from generations of local tradition.

Gerona

Isdaan Floating Restaurant

Step into a whimsical dining village where floating bamboo huts meet larger-than-life statues and playful attractions. Isdaan offers not just a feast of authentic Filipino seafood, but a vibrant cultural experience right in the heart of Gerona.

Beyond its mouthwatering grilled dishes and rich heritage soups, the restaurant is famous for its "Tacsiyapo" wall—a fun, interactive space where guests can smash plates to release their stress. It's the perfect vibrant pitstop for passing travelers and families.

Anao

Pancit Anao

A noodle dish unlike any other in the province — thin rice noodles tossed in an amber-colored sauce, topped with fresh vegetables and a hard-boiled egg. Sold fresh every morning around the town plaza, it's the dish Anao locals bring home whenever they visit family in other towns.

Anao

Bibingkang Labong

A rare and unique local delicacy, Bibingkang Labong offers an inventive twist on the traditional Filipino rice cake by incorporating tender bamboo shoots (labong). The young bamboo adds a subtle, earthy flavor and a pleasing crunch that perfectly balances the rich, creamy sweetness of the coconut milk and glutinous rice. Baked to a golden perfection, this distinct savory-sweet treat stands as a testament to the town's resourcefulness and rich culinary heritage.

Anao

Kamnar

A neighborhood favorite for merienda and evening get-togethers, Kamnar pairs home-style Tarlaqueño dishes with ice-cold kamias and calamansi coolers — the kind of unpretentious spot where regulars are greeted by name.

Bamban

Cafe Sou

Nestled in the municipality of Bamban, Cafe Sou offers a cozy, modern retreat for locals and travelers alike. Enjoy their generous spread of hearty home-cooked meals, perfectly plated viands, and refreshing signature iced drinks in a bright, relaxing atmosphere.

La Paz

Yves

A cafe that has become La Paz's go-to spot for coffee runs and casual dinners alike, serving specialty iced coffee alongside plated pasta and grilled mains — a comfortable, air-conditioned break from a day of exploring the town.

La Paz

Kusina ni Inang

True to its name, Kusina ni Inang offers the comforting embrace of authentic home cooking right in the heart of La Paz. Known for its rich, hearty heritage dishes and warm hospitality, this beloved local eatery serves up generous, traditional meals that taste exactly like they were lovingly prepared in a mother's kitchen.

Victoria

Herbs 'N Slice Pizza

Located along Rizal Street, Herbs 'N Slice Pizza House offers a cozy, inviting retreat for locals and passing travelers alike. Known as a beloved hometown favorite in Victoria, this charming pizzeria serves up comforting, freshly baked signature pizzas alongside their refreshing, colorful house drinks. Whether you are grabbing a quick slice or settling in for a relaxed afternoon meal, its warm atmosphere and hearty flavors make it a perfect, welcoming stopover.

San Clemente

Dueg View Deck Cafe

Perched along a quiet hillside in San Clemente, Dueg View Deck Cafe pairs comforting all-day breakfast plates and hearty rice meals with sweeping views of the surrounding greenery — a laid-back deck where the food is just as good as the view.
