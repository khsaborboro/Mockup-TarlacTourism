Municipality of Anao

Dubbed as the “Ylang-Ylang Capital of the Philippines,” Anao has effortlessly established itself as a preferred choice for travelers seeking a serene agricultural respite and to experience a plethora of eco-tourism attractions, verdant parks, fragrant essential oil extraction industries, and profound historical sites.

As the smallest municipality in Tarlac, Anao takes immense pride in its clean, green environment and was recognized for its exemplary community-based heritage preservation and agricultural prowess.

Ylang-Ylang Festival

Recognized for its fragrant natural surroundings, Anao presents an ideal destination for visitors seeking an immersive experience of the Ylang-Ylang Festival. This celebration takes place annually, honoring the town's primary livelihood and the beautiful blossoms that line its streets.

This festival incorporates numerous traditional Filipino elements, including vibrant street performances, agricultural fairs, and a showcase of locally produced essential oils, soaps, and perfumes. It serves as a beautiful tribute to the hardworking farmers and the distinct identity of Anao.

Anao Town Plaza

Anao Town Plaza, one of the municipality's premier attractions, stands as a testament to the town's commitment to the environment, perfected with lush greenery and centuries-old trees. Engage in rejuvenating activities such as afternoon strolls or simple relaxation to savor the plaza's captivating and peaceful atmosphere.

St. John Nepomucene Parish

Anao’s most magnificent historical edifice, the St. John Nepomucene Parish, boasts a solemn interior adored by both locals and pilgrims. Perched majestically in the town proper, it offers a quiet sanctuary for visitors exploring the northern towns of Tarlac.

Renowned for its deep historical roots, the parish accommodates generations of faithful Tarlaqueños, remaining a central pillar to the town's cultural and spiritual heritage.

Ylang-Ylang Extraction Center

Situated at the heart of the municipality, the Ylang-Ylang Extraction Center is a must-visit for anyone intrigued by the town's title. The facility showcases the meticulous process of extracting high-grade essential oils from freshly harvested Ylang-Ylang flowers.

From its viewing areas, visitors can observe local artisans and agricultural workers transform raw petals into world-class perfumes, soaps, and massage oils, which are also available for purchase as premium souvenirs.
