Mt. Pinatubo Trekking

Mt. Pinatubo Base Camp - Brgy. Santa Juliana, Capas, Tarlac

Rising from history and nature’s raw power, Mount Pinatubo is an iconic destination that offers an unforgettable adventure. Known for its legendary 1991 eruption, this once-devastating volcano has transformed into a breathtaking wonder, featuring a stunning turquoise crater lake surrounded by dramatic cliffs and rugged landscapes.

Hiking to Mount Pinatubo is an experience like no other. The trek takes you through lahar-carved canyons, scenic trails, and unique rock formations, leading to the awe-inspiring crater lake—an ideal spot for sightseeing and photography. Whether you’re an adventure seeker or a nature lover, this journey promises both thrill and beauty. Embark on the ultimate adventure and witness the majesty of Mount Pinatubo!

Canarem Lake Bird Sanctuary

Victoria, Tarlac

Canarem Lake Bird Sanctuary, located in the municipality of Victoria, Tarlac, is a vital wetland and a thriving haven for wildlife. This expansive lake acts as a critical refuge for thousands of migratory and endemic birds, including egrets, herons, and wild ducks, which flock to the area to seek shelter and food.

Surrounded by lush vegetation and tranquil waters, the sanctuary offers a peaceful retreat for bird watchers, nature photographers, and eco-tourists. Whether you're catching the stunning sunset reflections on the lake or observing the rich biodiversity, Canarem Lake provides a truly immersive experience with nature.

Capas National Shrine



Capas National Shrine

Standing as a solemn tribute to the bravery and sacrifice of Filipino and American soldiers during World War II, the Capas National Shrine commemorates the harrowing history of the Death March. Located in Capas, Tarlac, it honors those who endured unimaginable hardship and those who perished along the march. The shrine features a majestic monument, as well as peaceful grounds where visitors can reflect on the courage and resilience of the fallen heroes. The site serves as a poignant reminder of the sacrifices made for freedom and peace.

In its center, there is a three-pronged pillar terminating into a sharpened peak. A seventy-meter concrete obelisk soaring above the trees and pointing to the great blue sky.

The shrine also features beautifully landscaped gardens, walking paths, and informative displays about the historical events of the Bataan Death March. Notably, the site includes a museum where artifacts and exhibits help tell the story of the courageous soldiers who endured the march. The peaceful atmosphere of the grounds invites visitors to pay their respects while learning about one of the darkest yet most important chapters in Philippine history. Monasterio De Tarlac

Monasterio de Tarlac, San Jose, Tarlac

The Monasterio de Tarlac is a Benedictine monastery that was established in 2004. It serves as a peaceful spiritual retreat and pilgrimage site, notably housing a Relic of the True Cross, a fragment believed to be from the cross of Jesus Christ. The monastery's simple yet striking architecture blends with its natural surroundings, offering visitors a place for reflection and faith. It stands as a symbol of the Philippines' Catholic heritage and devotion.

At the Monasterio de Tarlac, visitors can enjoy a peaceful blend of spiritual reflection and natural beauty. One of the main highlights is the Relic of the True Cross, which pilgrims can venerate, adding a deeply spiritual dimension to their visit. The monastery offers a serene environment for prayer, meditation, and quiet contemplation, allowing guests to reconnect with their faith. It provides stunning panoramic views of Tarlac’s lush landscape, with designated viewpoints perfect for photography or simply taking in the tranquility of the area.

Mt. Kawayan

Mt. Kawayan - San Jose, Tarlac

Mt. Kawayan, nestled in the scenic municipality of San Jose, Tarlac, is an emerging eco-tourism destination perfect for outdoor enthusiasts and nature lovers. Named after the abundant bamboo (kawayan) groves that line its trails, the mountain features rolling hills, lush greenery, and sweeping panoramic views of the Tarlac mountain ranges. It offers a refreshing and tranquil escape from the bustling city.

The trek to Mt. Kawayan is a rewarding adventure, featuring well-shaded forested paths, occasional river crossings, and scenic ascents. At the summit, hikers are treated to a breathtaking vantage point and a cool, relaxing breeze. Whether you are seeking a challenging hike, a spot for landscape photography, or a peaceful camping trip, Mt. Kawayan provides a perfect backdrop for an unforgettable nature experience.

Mt. Tangisan

Mt. Tangisan - Iba, San Jose, Tarlac

Located in Brgy. Iba, San Jose, Tarlac, Mt. Tangisan is a favorite destination for hikers, trail runners, and 4x4 off-road enthusiasts. The name "Tangisan" locally translates to "a place of weeping," historically referring to the steep, challenging trails that made early climbers cry. Today, it stands as a thrilling outdoor adventure, featuring rugged terrain, river crossings, and expansive grasslands.

Reaching the summit rewards adventurers with stunning, unobstructed panoramic views of the Tarlac mountain ranges and the vast plains below. It is a fantastic spot for a day hike, overnight camping, or catching a breathtaking sunrise. Its close proximity to other natural attractions in San Jose makes it a perfect addition to any Central Luzon eco-tourism itinerary.

Mt. Damas

Mt. Damas - San Clemente, Tarlac

Mt. Damas, located in San Clemente, Tarlac, is renowned among mountaineers for its challenging trails and rewarding scenery. Standing at roughly 685 meters above sea level, it offers a thrilling day hike or an overnight adventure. The trail features a dynamic mix of river crossings, steep assaults, and rope segments, making it a favorite for hikers seeking an adrenaline-pumping outdoor experience.

Reaching the summit provides hikers with a breathtaking panoramic view of the Tarlac plains and neighboring mountains. A popular side trip during the descent is the stunning Ubod Falls, where climbers can cool off in its refreshing waters. With its diverse terrain and pristine natural beauty, Mt. Damas stands out as one of Central Luzon's premier eco-tourism destinations.

02 — Before You Go

Good to know

Book ahead

ATV and paramotor slots fill up on weekends — arrange with a certified local operator before you arrive.

Dress for the trail

Closed-toe shoes and quick-dry clothing for lahar crossings, mud, and river wading on the Pinatubo route.

Start early

Pinatubo and Damas treks begin at dawn to beat the midday heat — coordinate pickup times with your guide.

Best season

Dry months make river crossings safer and trails firmer — check conditions before a rainy-season visit.
