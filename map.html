04 — The Province At A Glance

Discover Tarlac

Often referred to as the "Melting Pot of Central Luzon," Tarlac is known for its diverse population. The province is home to a mix of several ethnic groups, primarily the Kapampangans in the south, Ilocanos and Pangasinenses in the north, and Tagalogs near the border with Nueva Ecija.
